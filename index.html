Examples

Three websites you can actually try

These aren’t pictures. Click any one and a real, working website opens — you can click its menu and move around it just like a visitor would. Pick whichever feels closest to what you want, and we’ll build yours from there.

3 pages

Bold, with big photos

A small website with three pages: the front page, your menu, and a page where people book a table. Dark and photo-led, so the food does the selling. Good for restaurants, cafés and bars.

  • “Book a table” button
  • Customer reviews

5 pages

Calm and simple

Five pages: the front page, what you offer, about you, a booking page, and how to find you. Light and uncluttered, with plenty of breathing room. Good for salons, studios and small shops.

  • “Book a visit” button
  • Customer reviews

6 pages

Detailed and businesslike

A bigger website, six pages: the front page, how you work, what you offer, past projects, a place to post updates, and contact. Room to explain things properly. Good for consultants and small software companies.

  • Shows your numbers
  • Client reviews

More pages, fewer pages, a different look — all of it can change. These three are a starting point, not a menu.
